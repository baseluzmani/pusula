# Portfolio

Your investments: what you hold, what it is worth, what you have made, and where you enter trades. Money is in pounds unless a column says otherwise.

## Portfolio

Every holding with its value today in pounds, and how that has changed since a past date you pick.

Choose a date in "Compare with" to add the change since that evening. The dates come from the nightly snapshot. A holding you have sold since that date still shows, at zero, so the totals add up.

Terms: value, snapshot, asset-type

### Holdings detail {#holdings-detail}

One row per holding: units, today's price in its own currency, value in pounds and share of the total. With a comparison date, also the value then and the change.

### By category {#by-category}

The total split by category: the company for a single share, the sector or region for a fund.

### By asset type {#by-asset-type}

The total split by asset type, the broad theme such as Tech-AI, Gold or Fund.

### By account {#by-account}

The total split by the account each holding sits in.

## P&L

Profit and loss for each holding in pounds: what you have made or lost so far, including sales and dividends.

Open holdings show by default. "Show closed" adds holdings you have sold completely. "Compact view" keeps only prices and recent returns. The return columns are shaded so the best and worst movers stand out.

Terms: pnl, net-cost, roce, roce-annualised, period-returns

### P&L table {#table}

One row per holding. The bottom row adds up value, P&L and today's move in pounds.

## P&L ROCI

The return on the money you actually had invested in each holding, and for how long.

Making £1,000 on £10,000 held for a month is better than making it over three years. This tab shows that difference. Tick rows to see them as one combined basket. It always covers your whole history; there is no date filter.

Terms: capital-employed, pnl, fx-pnl, roce, roce-annualised, volatility, max-drawdown, sharpe, net-cost

### Holdings table {#table}

One row per holding; closed ones on request. "Last buy" and "Last sell" show how long ago that trade was and how the price has moved since. Holdings held for under 180 days are greyed out, because a yearly figure from a short stretch is misleading.

### Basket summary {#basket}

The ticked rows treated as one holding: money invested, P&L and return for the group as a whole.

### Capital employed chart {#capital-chart}

How much money was invested over time, at what you paid.

### Validation {#validation}

Self-checks that the figures add up. A failed check means something in the data needs a look.

## Transactions

Every trade you have entered, with what each one is worth today. Filter by account, vehicle, fund, date and type.

Terms: cost-gbp, value, line-pnl, vehicle

### Transactions table {#table}

Buys show as money out (a negative cost), sells and dividends as money in. The count above the table and the TOTAL row cover only what the filters show.

## Accounts

What sits in each account, with its cash and its own total.

Units per account come from the trades booked to that account. The P&L shown is for the whole holding, not just the part in that account, because cost is tracked per holding.

Terms: value, pnl

## Charts

Pictures of the portfolio: how it is split today and how it has grown.

### Portfolio breakdown {#breakdown}

Inner ring: asset type. Outer ring: category. The mortgage is taken off the house, so property shows what you own net.

### Net worth history {#networth}

Total value over time, from the nightly snapshots.

### Portfolio value by category {#by-category}

Value over time, stacked by category. Small categories are grouped as Other.

### Composition {#composition}

Each category's share of the total over time, without property. It shows how the mix shifts, not whether the total grew.

## Summary

A short list of every holding by value, in thousands of pounds, with the change since a past snapshot.

Terms: value, snapshot

### Summary table {#table}

One row per holding with its share of the total. Pick a date in "Compare with" to add the value then and the change.

## Inputs

Where you add and correct trades and keep cash balances up to date. The only Portfolio tab that changes data.

Once you pick a fund, account, date and type, the price, FX rate and commission fill themselves in; you can change them. On a sell, the quantity fills in with what that account holds.

Terms: fx-rate

### Add transaction {#form}

A new trade, or the one you picked to edit. The FX rate is the one on the trade date and fixes the trade's cost in pounds for good.

### Recent transactions {#recent}

The latest trades. Edit or delete one from here.

### Cash {#cash}

Cash balances per account and currency.

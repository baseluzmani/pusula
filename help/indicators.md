# Indicators

Market background: interest rates, fear and volatility, and commodities. The boxes at the top of each tab are the readings worth seeing first.

## Rates

US and UK interest rates: the Treasury yield curve, the gap between short and long rates, real yields and inflation expectations, gilts, and corporate credit spreads.

## Volatility

How nervous markets are: VIX, MOVE for bonds, the CNN Fear & Greed score, the S&P 500 and the dollar.

## Commodity

Oil, gold, silver, copper and gas, plus ratios that hint at the economic mood, such as gold against copper and gold miners against gold.

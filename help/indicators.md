# Indicators

The market background: interest rates, fear and volatility, and commodities. The boxes along the top of each tab are the readings worth seeing first; a box is red when the move is the worrying kind.

## Rates

US and UK interest rates: where they are, how the yield curve is shaped, what inflation the market expects, and how much extra lenders charge risky companies.

Terms: yield-curve, curve-inversion, real-yield, breakeven, credit-spread

### Treasury curve {#treasury-curve}

US government borrowing rates for 2, 5, 10 and 30 years over time, with recessions shaded.

### Gilt curve {#gilt-curve}

UK government borrowing rates for 5 to 30 years, with Bank Rate underneath.

### Recession spreads {#recession-spreads}

Long rates minus short rates. Below zero, the curve is inverted, which has often come before a recession.

### Real vs nominal {#real-vs-nominal}

The US 10-year rate before and after inflation. The gap between them is the inflation the market expects.

### Gilt real & breakeven {#gilt-real}

The same idea for the UK, from index-linked gilts.

### Curve shape {#curve-shape}

The whole US curve on a few chosen dates, to compare its shape.

### Curve evolution {#curve-evolution}

One curve per quarter, showing how the shape has changed.

### All series {#all-series}

Every rate and spread collected, including ones not charted elsewhere. Click a row to chart it.

## Volatility

How nervous markets are, in shares and in bonds.

Terms: vix, vix-term, move, fear-greed

### VIX {#vix}

Expected swings in US shares over the next month, against its range over the past year. Above 20 usually means nervous.

### VIX term structure {#vix-term}

Near-term fear against three-month fear. Above 1 means short-term stress.

### MOVE {#move}

Expected swings in US government bonds: the bond market's VIX.

### Fear & Greed {#fear-greed}

CNN's 0 to 100 mood score, built from seven market signals. Below 25 is extreme fear; above 75 extreme greed.

### Equity {#equity}

Main share indices, all starting from the same date, with their recent returns.

## Commodity

Oil, gold, silver, copper and gas, and the ratios between them that hint at the economic mood.

Terms: gold-copper, miners-gold

### Commodity signals {#signals}

Prices, ratios and spreads that say more than prices alone, such as Brent against US oil.

### Copper / gold and the 10Y {#copper-gold}

Copper over gold tends to move with the US 10-year rate. When the two part ways, one of them is likely wrong.

### Miners vs gold {#miners-gold}

Gold miners against gold. Rising means the miners back up gold's move.

### Your themes {#themes}

Your themes (for example uranium or semiconductors) each against the thing that drives them.

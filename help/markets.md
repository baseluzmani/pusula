# Markets

Prices and trends for any group of instruments. Returns are in each instrument's own currency, so a US share is measured in dollars.

The Universe bar at the top picks the group for every Markets tab: your holdings, everything tracked, one asset type, the contents of one ETF, or a hand-picked set.

## Transactions

A price chart for one instrument with your buys and sells marked on it, monthly returns below, and a returns table on the right. Click a row to chart it.

Instruments you have never traded show a plain price line.

Terms: universe, period-returns

### Returns table {#returns-table}

Each instrument's return over periods from one day to "Since" a date you choose, grouped by asset type. Click a row to show it in the chart. In the ETF universe the fund comes first, then its holdings with their weight in the fund.

### Price {#price}

The price over time, with an up arrow for each buy and a down arrow for each sell.

### Monthly returns {#monthly}

The return of each month, one row per year, coloured green to red.

## Compare

Several instruments on one chart, all starting from zero on the same date, so you can see which did better.

Terms: universe, rebased

### Returns table {#returns-table}

Returns over several periods for the whole group. Tick rows to add or remove their lines on the chart.

### Rebased returns {#chart}

Each ticked instrument's return since the chosen date. Everything starts at zero, whatever its price.

### Returns of plotted lines {#plotted}

The same returns, for just the lines on the chart.

## Heatmap

A grid of returns over several periods, coloured from red to green, with a day-move map underneath.

Terms: universe, period-returns

### Returns grid {#grid}

One row per instrument, one column per period. Sort by any period; "Group by type" splits the rows by asset type.

### Latest day {#latest-day}

One tile per holding, sized by its value and coloured by its latest daily move. A fund that has not priced today shows its last move, with the date on the tile. In the ETF universe the tiles are the fund's holdings, sized by their weight.

## Momentum

Where each instrument sits on its long trend against its recent move, both measured against its own normal day-to-day swings.

Pick a horizon (short, medium, long or tactical) and, if you like, a benchmark to measure against. Dots you hold are coloured, and bigger dots are bigger holdings. Click a dot to show its trail.

Terms: universe, momentum-score, momentum-quadrants

### Momentum map {#map}

Right of centre: the long trend is up. Above centre: the recent move is up. Scores within about half a point of zero are just noise; above 1 is a real move. The table below lists the same scores.

## Monitor

For each instrument: how far it is below its high of the past year, where it sits against its long-term average, how close it is to your own buy, sell and review levels, and a status.

Click a row to see its one-year chart and to set or change its levels.

Terms: universe, drawdown-52w, moving-averages, trend-phase, next-cross, monitor-status, levels

### Monitor table {#table}

One row per instrument with its fall from the 52-week high, its position against the 50- and 200-day averages, your levels and its status. Status thresholds can be changed in Data → Config.

### Instrument detail {#detail}

The one-year chart for the row you clicked, with your levels and trades marked, and the form to set its buy, sell and review levels.

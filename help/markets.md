# Markets

Prices and trends for any group of instruments: your holdings, everything tracked, one asset type, or the contents of one ETF. Returns are in each instrument's own currency.

## Transactions

A price chart for one instrument with your buys and sells marked on it, monthly returns below, and a returns table on the right. Click a row to chart it.

## Compare

Several instruments on one chart, all starting from zero on the same date, so you can see which did better. Tick rows in the table to add or remove lines.

## Heatmap

A grid of returns over several periods, coloured from red to green, with a day-move map underneath.

## Momentum

Where each instrument sits on its trend against its recent move, both measured against its own normal swings. The four corners show leaders, fading, recovering and laggards.

## Monitor

How far each instrument is from its 52-week high and its 200-day average, from the buy, sell and review levels you set, and when its trend lines may cross.

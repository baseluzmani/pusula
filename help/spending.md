# Spending

Household spending from bank and card statements, sorted into categories.

## Overview

A year of spending by category and month, as a table or a stacked chart. Card repayments and transfers (Finance) are left out by default.

## Transactions

Every bank line, with filters. Change a category, add a note, split a line into parts, or send lines back to the Manual Queue.

## Statements

Each uploaded statement, card by card, and whether it adds up: opening balance plus money in, minus money out, equals closing balance.

## Manual Queue

Lines no rule could place, mostly Amazon, PayPal and cash. Pick a category for each, one by one or in bulk.

## Import

Upload bank statements (PDF) or CSV exports. Lines are matched with what is already stored, so nothing is counted twice.

## Rules

The keywords that put bank lines into categories automatically, the cards, and saved split templates.

## Allowances

Pension allowance used and left this tax year, including unused allowance carried forward and the high-income taper.

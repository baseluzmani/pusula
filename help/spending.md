# Spending

Household spending from bank and card statements, sorted into categories. Money going out shows in brackets; a refund in a category reduces it.

## Overview

A year of spending by category and month, as a table or a stacked chart. Click a category to open its sub-categories.

Income and pending lines are shown only if you ask, and never count as spending. Finance (card repayments and transfers) is left out by default so nothing is counted twice. A split line counts in each of its parts.

Terms: split, pending

### Spending by month {#table}

Categories down the side, months across, a total for each. Switch to the chart for the same figures as stacked bars.

## Transactions

Every stored bank line, with filters. Tick lines to change their category, add a note, split them into parts, or send them back to the Manual Queue. Export downloads exactly what the filters show.

A line from a foreign card shows the original amount too. "Provisional" means it came from a CSV and its statement has not been uploaded yet.

Terms: rule, split, provisional

### Transactions table {#table}

One row per bank line, with its card, category, note and statement. Split lines show their parts, and the category filter and totals use the parts.

## Statements

Each uploaded statement, card by card, and whether it adds up.

Click a statement to see its totals, its checks, its spending by category and its lines.

Terms: reconciled

### Coverage {#coverage}

Each card's months at a glance: a tick when that month's statement adds up, ! when it does not, ? when it is missing, and amber when a month has lines on no statement yet.

### Statements table {#table}

One row per statement: opening and closing balance, money in and out, number of lines and its status, with the reason if it does not add up.

### Spending by category {#by-category}

What the selected statement spent, by category.

## Manual Queue

Lines no rule could place, mostly Amazon, PayPal and cash. Give each a category, one by one or many at once.

Only lines since December 2024 are shown unless you tick "Include older".

Terms: pending, rule, split

### One by one {#one-by-one}

The newest pending line with one-click suggestions: how you placed similar lines before, your split templates, and your most-used categories. "Remember" saves a rule so the same line is placed automatically next time.

### All pending lines {#all}

Every pending line in a table. Filter, tick and assign a category to many at once.

## Import

Upload bank statements (PDF) or CSV exports, several at once. Each line is matched with what is already stored, so nothing is counted twice and categories you set by hand are kept.

Before anything is saved, the preview shows what is new, what it paired with a stored line, and what it would delete because the statement does not have it.

Terms: rule

### Import preview {#preview}

The lines in the files you chose and what will happen to each one. Exclude any you want left alone, then Import.

### Cards {#cards}

Each card with its latest line and statement, how many days since, and any gaps. "Due" means it is time to upload again.

## Rules

How imported lines get their category automatically, and the lists behind it.

Terms: rule, split

### Keyword rules {#keyword-rules}

If a line's description contains the keyword, it gets that category. The first matching rule wins. A rule can also need an amount, which is how regular Amazon, PayPal and cash lines are placed. Below the form, a preview shows which stored lines the rule would change.

### Categories {#categories}

Add or remove categories and sub-categories. Removing one does not change stored lines; re-map them first.

### Re-map transactions {#remap}

Move every line from one category to another.

### Split templates {#templates}

Saved ways to split a line, such as a cash withdrawal into cleaning and tutor, and how often each was used.

### Cards {#cards}

Rename a card or retire one you no longer use.

## Allowances

Pension and ISA allowances used and left in each tax year, for each person, plus the junior ISA.

Terms: carry-forward, taper

### Limits by tax year {#limits}

The official limits for each tax year.

### Inputs {#inputs}

Income and contributions per person and tax year. Pension contributions are entered as paid; tax relief is added on.

### Pension allowance and carry forward {#pension}

How much pension allowance each year had, how much was used, and the unused amount you can still carry forward.

### ISA {#isa}

ISA money paid in this tax year against the limit.

### Junior ISA {#jisa}

Junior ISA money paid in this tax year against its limit.

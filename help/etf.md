# ETF

What funds and ETFs hold, how that changes, and an estimate of today's move for funds that price late.

## Holdings

What a fund owns on a given date. A company held under several tickers counts once.

## Changes

What the manager bought, sold, added to and trimmed between two dates.

## Compare

Where two funds own the same companies, and how much money overlaps.

## Fund compare

Any two sets of holdings side by side: an ETF, a pension fund, or a pension blend.

## Day estimate

An estimate of today's move for funds that price late, such as the pension funds, worked out from what they hold and live prices. A backtest shows how far to trust it.

## Ticker map

The table that links each holding's tickers and codes to one company record. Most other ETF screens depend on it.

## Sources

Where each fund's holdings file comes from.
